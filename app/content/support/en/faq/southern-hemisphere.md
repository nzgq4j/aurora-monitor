---
title: Does Polar Lights work in the southern hemisphere?
summary: Yes. The Tonight verdict, alerts and saved locations work anywhere, including for the aurora australis.
order: 70
---
Yes. The southern lights (aurora australis) follow the same rules as the northern lights. The **Tonight** verdict, the chance score, alerts and saved locations all work for southern places, using your distance from the southern magnetic pole.

The small map on the Now page shows the northern oval. When your place is in the southern hemisphere it says so under the map.
