---
title: How do I cancel Chaser?
summary: It depends on how you pay. Card payments are managed in Polar Lights, Google Play payments in Google Play.
order: 50
---
## Paid by card (on the website)

On a phone, open **You → Plan & billing**. On a computer, open **Dashboard → Account**. Then choose **Manage billing** or **Cancel membership**. Stripe's secure billing page opens, where you can cancel; it shows the date your membership ends.

## Paid through Google Play

Open **You → Plan & billing** and choose **Manage in Google Play**. Or, in the Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then **Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Polar Lights → Cancel subscription**.

![Plan & billing in You, with Manage in Google Play ringed](screen:you-billing)

> Deleting your account also cancels a card subscription straight away, and stops a Google Play subscription from renewing.
