---
title: When do my badges and points appear?
summary: About an hour after a report, once it has been confirmed.
order: 30
---
A sighting is checked **about an hour after you report it**, so that others nearby have had time to report too. It counts when either:

- **another member** reported aurora within 50 km and an hour of you, or
- **NOAA's OVATION forecast** showed at least a 10% chance of aurora near you.

Confirmed reports earn points and badges. Checks run every 15 minutes, and a *New badge* message appears the next time you open Polar Lights. Your badges and rank are under **You → Badges**.
