---
title: Why does it say “Unlikely” when Kp is high?
summary: The Tonight verdict is for your place, so darkness, cloud and the Moon count as much as Kp.
order: 10
---
Kp measures how active the aurora is around the whole planet. The **Tonight** card answers a narrower question: will *you* see it, where you are, in the next few hours? It combines four things:

- **Activity at your latitude.** Each place needs a certain Kp before the aurora reaches it. A place at 55° magnetic latitude needs about Kp 4, and one further from the pole needs more.
- **Darkness.** The aurora can't be seen in daylight or bright twilight.
- **Cloud.** Thick cloud hides it, however strong it is.
- **The Moon.** A bright Moon washes out fainter displays.

The line under the verdict names the main reason, such as *Activity too low*, *Too light*, *Cloudy* or *Bright Moon*. Check the **Next 12 hours** bars on the same card: a better hour later in the night is often shown there.
