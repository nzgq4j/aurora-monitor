---
title: How do I delete my account or some of my data?
summary: Delete everything from You or the Dashboard, or remove only some of your data.
order: 60
---
- **In the Android app:** open **You** and choose **Delete account** at the bottom.
- **On the website:** sign in and open **Dashboard → Account**, then choose **Delete my account**.

![Delete account at the bottom of You](screen:you-delete)

Your account and its data are deleted straight away and you're signed out. You can also keep your account and remove only some data: saved locations, gallery photos or alerts. [Delete your account](/delete-account) explains how, what else you can ask us to remove, and exactly what is deleted.
