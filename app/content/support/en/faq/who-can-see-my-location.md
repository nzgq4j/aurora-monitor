---
title: Can other people see my location?
summary: Your saved locations are private. Sightings and gallery photos show only a rounded place, or none.
order: 40
---
- **Saved locations** are private to your account. Only you see them.
- **Sightings** appear on the map for 24 hours at a position rounded to about 10 km, with nothing that identifies you. They're deleted after 30 days.
- **Gallery photos** show the place only as precisely as you choose when posting: a town (to about 10 km), a wider area, or no place at all. Location data stored inside the photo file is removed before upload.

The [privacy policy](/privacy) explains everything we store and why.
