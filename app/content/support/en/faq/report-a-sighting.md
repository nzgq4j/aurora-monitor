---
title: Why can't I report a sighting yet?
summary: “I saw it” opens once it's dark at the place on the Tonight card, and you can report once every 12 hours.
order: 20
---
The **I saw it** button on the Tonight card works when:

- **It's dark** at the place shown on the card. Until then it says *You can report a sighting once it's dark here.*
- **You're signed in.** Reports are linked to your account so they can count towards your badges.
- **You haven't reported in the last 12 hours.** One report covers a whole night.

![The I saw it button, waiting until it's dark](screen:tonight-saw-it)

Reports appear on the map for 3 hours, without anything that identifies you, and help other members nearby.
