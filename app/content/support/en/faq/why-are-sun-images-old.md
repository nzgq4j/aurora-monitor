---
title: Why are some Sun images an hour old?
summary: Each spacecraft publishes on its own schedule. The time on each image shows when it was taken (in UT).
order: 80
---
The Sun views come straight from NASA's SDO, the ESA/NASA SOHO mission and NOAA's GOES-19 and SWFO-L1 spacecraft. Each publishes new images on its own schedule:

- **SDO** images are usually less than an hour old.
- **SOHO's LASCO coronagraphs** often lag by an hour or more.
- **NOAA's SUVI and CCOR** images update every few minutes to every 15 minutes.

The time printed on each image is when it was taken, in Universal Time (UT). The time-lapse saves one image of each view every hour.
