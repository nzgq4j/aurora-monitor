---
title: Alert emails don't arrive
summary: Check your spam folder, add the sending address to your contacts, and make sure Email is turned on.
order: 30
---
## Check spam

Alert emails come from **alerts@polarlights.io**. Look in your spam or junk folder, and in tabs such as *Promotions* or *Updates*. If you find one there, mark it as not spam and add **alerts@polarlights.io** to your contacts, so the next one reaches your inbox.

## Check the address and the switch

Open **Alerts**. Under **Deliver via**, **Email** should be on, and the address shown under it is where alerts go: your account's email address.

## Was an alert sent?

**Recent alerts**, on the Alerts page, lists every alert sent to you. If nothing is listed, no alert was sent: see [I didn't get an aurora alert](/support/troubleshooting/no-aurora-alert).

## Account emails

The confirmation link when you create an account and the password reset link come from the account service, not from **alerts@polarlights.io**. If one doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check spam, then ask for it again. If you're told there are too many attempts, wait a few minutes first.
