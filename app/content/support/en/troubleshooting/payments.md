## Paid through Google Play

- **"Google Play is still confirming your payment."** Some payment methods take a while. Chaser switches on as soon as Google confirms it.
- **"The purchase didn't go through."** If Google Play did charge you, Chaser is applied automatically the next time you open the app while signed in.
- **"This Google account already has a Chaser subscription."** The subscription belongs to the Polar Lights account that bought it. Sign in with that account.
- **"Memberships can't be bought in this version of the app."** Update Polar Lights from Google Play.

To see or cancel the subscription, open **You** → **Plan & billing** → **Manage in Google Play**.

## Paid by card on the website

Chaser usually switches on within a few seconds of finishing Stripe's checkout. If it doesn't, reload the page. **Checkout was cancelled. Nothing was charged.** means the payment wasn't completed; you can start again from **Plans**.

## A payment didn't go through later

If your plan shows that the last payment failed, update your card (**Manage billing**) or your Google Play payment method (**Manage in Google Play**) to keep Chaser.

## Signed in to the right account?

Chaser belongs to the account that paid for it. If you have more than one account, for example one with Google and one with a password, check which one you're signed in with in **You**.

If Chaser still isn't on, [contact support](/support/troubleshooting/contact-support) and tell us roughly when you paid and how.
