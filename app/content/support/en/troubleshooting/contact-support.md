---
title: Contact support
summary: Send us a message from the app or from any help page; we reply to your account's email address.
order: 100
---
If the help centre didn't solve it, send us a message:

- **In the app:** open **You** → **Report an issue or request a feature**.
- **On any help page:** use **Still need help?** at the bottom.

You need to be signed in, so we can reply to your account's email address. It helps to say:

- what you were trying to do and what happened instead,
- any message on screen, word for word,
- your device and whether you use the Android app, an iPhone Home Screen app or a browser. **You** → **Version** at the bottom shows your app version.

For anything about your personal data or deleting your account, write to **privacy@polarlights.io**.
