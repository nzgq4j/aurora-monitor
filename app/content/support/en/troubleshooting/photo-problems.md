## It says "Waiting for review"

Every photo is checked by a moderator before it appears in the gallery. Until then only you can see it, in **My gallery**. Photos that aren't aurora or the night sky, or aren't the poster's own, aren't approved.

## "You can post 10 photos a day."

That's the daily limit. Try again tomorrow.

## "The photo couldn't be posted."

- Check your connection and try again.
- The gallery takes JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIC photos. Some browsers can't open HEIC, the format iPhones use by default. If a HEIC photo fails, export it as JPEG first, or set **Settings** → **Camera** → **Formats** → **Most Compatible** on the iPhone for future photos.
- Make sure you ticked that you took the photo and that it follows the community rules.

## The place is wrong or missing

The place comes from where you choose under **Show where it was taken as**. Choose **Other** to search for the right town, or **Don't show a place**. Location data inside the file itself is removed before upload, so it can't be used.

## The photo disappeared

A photo reported by three members is hidden until a moderator reviews it. You can delete your own photos at any time with **Delete photo**; the points they earned are kept.
