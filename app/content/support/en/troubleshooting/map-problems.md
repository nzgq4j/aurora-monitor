---
title: The map or globe is blank or slow
summary: Reload, check your connection, switch to the flat map if the 3D globe won't start, and turn on the Aurora layer.
order: 50
---
## Nothing shows on the map

- Reload the page, or close and reopen the app.
- Check your connection. When you're offline the map shows **Offline · showing the last saved map data**, and updates when you're back online.
- **Basemap failed to load** means the map background couldn't be fetched. Reload to try again.

## The aurora is missing

Open **Map layers** and check that **Aurora** is on. If the map says **Aurora data is unavailable right now**, NOAA's aurora forecast couldn't be reached. It comes back on its own, usually within minutes.

Quiet nights can also look empty: the aurora may be a thin ring close to the pole.

## The 3D globe won't start

**This browser can't show the 3D globe. Switch to 2D.** means your browser or device has 3D graphics (WebGL) turned off or unsupported. Open **Map view** and choose **Flat map**. Turning on hardware acceleration in your browser's settings often fixes it.

## It's slow

The globe and the **City lights** and **Clouds** layers need the most data and processing. On an older phone or a slow connection, use the **Flat map** and turn off layers you don't need.
