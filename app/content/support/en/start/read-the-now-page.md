The **Now** page answers two questions: what is the aurora doing right now, and is it worth going outside where you are tonight?

## The aurora now

The top of the page describes the aurora around the world in a few words, such as *Quiet* or *Watch the sky*, with the current **Kp** level and a short summary of the solar wind. The map below it, **Auroral oval**, shows where aurora is likely in the next 30 to 90 minutes, seen from above the North Pole. Your Home is marked on it.

![The top of the Now page: the current level, Kp and the auroral oval](screen:now-top)

**Kp** runs from 0 to 9 and measures how disturbed Earth's magnetic field is. The higher it is, the further from the pole the aurora reaches. Kp 5 and above is a geomagnetic storm.

## Tonight

The **Tonight** card is the verdict for your Home. It combines four things: how strong the aurora is forecast to be, cloud, darkness and the Moon.

- **The verdict:** *Go out now*, *Go out later*, *Possible* or *Unlikely*.
- **Chance score:** the same verdict as a number from 0 to 100.
- **The main reason:** the line below says what's holding it back, such as *Cloudy* or *Too light*.
- **Best time:** the best window in the next 12 hours, and where in the sky to look.
- **Next 12 hours:** the chance hour by hour. Tap a bar to see that hour's Kp, cloud and darkness.

![The Tonight card: verdict and chance score, the main reason and the next 12 hours](screen:tonight-card)

Times on the card are local to the place.

## Your sky

**Your sky** shows the four numbers behind the verdict, for right now:

- **Aurora:** the chance of aurora overhead or just poleward of you.
- **Cloud:** forecast cloud for the next hour, with a check from satellite images.
- **Dark:** whether the Sun is far enough below the horizon, and until when.
- **Needs:** the Kp your place usually needs before aurora is seen there.

Tap the **i** in a card's corner for a short, simple explanation of everything on it.

![Your sky: aurora, cloud, darkness and the Kp your place needs](screen:your-sky)

## The days ahead

On a phone, these cards are folded to a one-line summary; tap a title to open it.

- **Geomagnetic outlook:** the Kp forecast. **Daily peak** covers 3 days; Chaser adds **3-hourly** and **27-day**.
- **Storm outlook:** NOAA's chances of geomagnetic storms over the next three days, solar flares and radiation, and any coronal mass ejections (CMEs) heading for Earth.
- **Last 12 weeks** and **Best nights ahead:** how often your place has reached the Kp it needs, and the nights in NOAA's 27-day outlook that should. Only the best nights are listed, not every night.
