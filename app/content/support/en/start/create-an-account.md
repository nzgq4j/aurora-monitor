You can read the forecast without an account. You need one to:

- get aurora alerts by email or push,
- keep your saved places on every device,
- report a sighting with **I saw it**, post photos and earn badges.

The account is free. Chaser is an optional upgrade; see [Plans: Free and Chaser](/support/start/plans-and-chaser).

## Create an account

1. On a phone, open **You** and tap **Create account**. On a computer, choose **Sign in** at the top right, then **Create an account**.
2. Enter your name, email address and a password of at least 8 characters, then tap **Create account**.
3. Open the confirmation link we email you to finish.

Or tap **Google** to use your Google account instead of a password.

![The Create account form, with the Google button below it](screen:signin)

## Sign in on another device

Choose **Sign in** and use the same email and password, or **Google**. Places you saved on that device before signing in are added to your account, up to your plan's limit.

## Forgot your password?

On the sign-in form, choose **Forgot your password?**, enter your email and tap **Send reset link**. The link in the email lets you set a new password.
