## Night mode

Your eyes take about 20 minutes to get used to the dark, and a bright white screen undoes that in seconds. **Night mode** draws the whole app in dim red so it doesn't.

On a phone, open **You** → **Night mode** and choose:

- **Off**,
- **On**, or
- **Auto when dark**, which turns it on once it's dark where you are.

## Language

Polar Lights is in English, Spanish, French, German, Dutch, Norwegian, Danish, Swedish, Finnish and Russian. It starts in your device's language when it can.

To change it, open **You** → **Language** on a phone, or **Dashboard** → **Account** → **Language** on a computer. When you're signed in, your alert emails use the same language.
