## Points and ranks

You earn points for [confirmed sightings](/support/start/i-saw-it), for backing up another member's report, and for photos and the helpful votes they get. Points move you up the ranks:

| Rank | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Stargazer | 0 |
| Watcher | 100 |
| Spotter | 250 |
| Tracker | 500 |
| Storm Hunter | 1,000 |
| Aurora Hunter | 2,000 |
| Polar Legend | 4,000 |

The top of the page shows your rank and how many points you need for the next one.

## Badges

Badges are grouped into **Reporter**, **Storm Witness**, **Contributor**, **Explorer** and **Seasoned**. Many have tiers that you work through. Tap a badge to see what it's for and when you earned it.

Some badges stay secret until you earn them, and **Special events** has badges for particular storms and occasions.

> Badges appear about an hour after a report, once it's confirmed. See [When do badges appear?](/support/faq/when-do-badges-appear)

## Redeem a badge code

If you were given a badge code, tap **Redeem it** under **Have a badge code?**, enter the code and tap **Redeem**. Some codes are for Chaser members only.

![Redeem a code](screen:badges-redeem)

## Your gallery name

Your rank and badges are shown with your **Gallery name**. Tap **Change** next to it on the Badges page to choose another.
