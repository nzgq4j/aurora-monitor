When you can see the aurora, tap **I saw it** on the **Tonight** card. Your report shows on the map for members nearby, and helps the forecast for everyone.

![The I saw it button on the Tonight card](screen:tonight-saw-it)

## Report it

1. Make sure you're signed in.
2. On **Now**, tap **I saw it** on the **Tonight** card.
3. Choose **How bright?**: **Faint**, **Clear** or **Bright**. This is optional.

You'll see how many other people reported aurora nearby in the last hour.

## When you can report

- **It has to be dark** at the place on the card. Until then the button reads *You can report a sighting once it's dark here.*
- **One report every 12 hours.** One report covers the whole night.

## What happens next

- Reports show on the map's **Sightings** layer for 3 hours, without anything that identifies you.
- About an hour later your report is checked. It's **confirmed** when another member reports aurora within 50 km and an hour of you, or the forecast showed a real chance where you were.
- Confirmed reports earn points and badges. See [Badges and ranks](/support/start/badges-and-ranks).
