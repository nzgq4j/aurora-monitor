Polar Lights has two plans. **Free** is free for good. **Chaser** adds faster, wider alerts and the longer-range forecasts.

![The plans, with the billing cycle and what each includes](screen:plans)

## What each includes

| | Free | Chaser |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saved places | 1 | 10 |
| Aurora alerts | Email, for Home | Email and push, for all your places |
| 1-hour heads-up | No | Yes |
| Alerts before aurora (Bz, substorms, CMEs, flares) | No | Yes |
| Kp forecast | 3 days, daily peak | 3-hourly and 27-day |
| Solar activity graphs | No | Yes |

The forecast itself, the map, the Sun tab, sightings, the gallery and badges are the same on both.

## Start Chaser

On a phone, tap **Upgrade to Chaser** at the top of **You**. On a computer, open **Dashboard** → **Plans**. Choose **Monthly** or **Annual**, then **Choose Chaser**. The price is shown in your currency.

Your first subscription starts with a **7-day free trial**. Cancel before it ends and you aren't charged.

- **In the Android app,** you pay through Google Play.
- **On the website,** you pay by card through Stripe's secure checkout. Any tax that applies where you live is added there.

## Change or cancel

Open **You** → **Plan & billing** on a phone, or **Dashboard** → **Account** on a computer. If you pay through Google Play, choose **Manage in Google Play**; if you pay by card, choose **Manage billing**. See [How do I cancel Chaser?](/support/faq/cancel-chaser)
