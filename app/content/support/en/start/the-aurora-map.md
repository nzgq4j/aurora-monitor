**Map** shows where the aurora is likely right now, worldwide, so you can see how close it is to you and where to go for a better view.

![The aurora map, with the aurora and the layers panel](screen:map)

## Move around

Drag to move, and pinch or scroll to zoom. The house button zooms back to your Home.

## Check any spot

Tap anywhere on the map. The panel at the bottom shows that spot's chance of aurora, cloud and darkness, and its next hours. From there you can **Save location** or **Set as Home**. **Remove pin** clears it.

## Layers

Drag the panel up, or tap **Show more**, then tap **Map layers** to choose what the map shows:

- **Aurora:** the chance of aurora overhead.
- **Clouds:** infrared satellite images of cloud, updated every hour.
- **Night:** where it's dark, twilight or day. Aurora only shows where it's dark.
- **Best spots:** named towns where aurora is likely and it's dark and clear now. Tap one for details.
- **Sightings:** reports from members in the last 3 hours.
- **Saved spots:** your saved places.
- **City lights:** bright areas wash out faint aurora.

![The map layers: aurora, clouds, night, best spots, sightings, saved spots and city lights](screen:map-layers)

**Map view** switches between the **Flat map** and the **3D globe**. The globe shows the whole oval, with the night side and city lights.

## The last day

**Aurora over the last day** steps back through the last 24 hours of the aurora. Tap play to watch it, or **Live** to return to now.
