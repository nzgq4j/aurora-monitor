title: Get Polar Lights on your phone or computer
summary: Use the Android app, add the website to an iPhone's Home Screen, or open polarlights.io in any browser.
order: 10
---
Polar Lights works the same everywhere: in the Android app, on an iPhone or iPad, and in a web browser on a computer. Your account, saved places and alert settings follow you to every device you sign in on.

## Android

Install **Polar Lights** from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.polarlights.app). The app adds push alerts on your phone and Google Play billing for Chaser.

## iPhone and iPad

There's no App Store app. Add the website to your Home Screen instead, and it opens full screen like an app:

1. Open **polarlights.io** in Safari.
2. Tap **Share** in Safari's toolbar.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open Polar Lights from your Home Screen.

> Push alerts on an iPhone only work when Polar Lights is opened from the Home Screen, not from a Safari tab.

## Computer

Open **polarlights.io** in any up-to-date browser. On a computer the pages are in the bar at the top: **Now**, **Map**, **Gallery**, **Sun** and **Dashboard**. On a phone they're in the tab bar at the bottom, with **You** in place of Dashboard.
