The **Gallery** has members' aurora photos. Anyone can look; you need an account to post or vote.

## Browse

The buttons at the top choose which photos you see: **Tonight**, **This storm**, **Near me** (within 500 km of your Home), **Top**, **Recent** and, once you're signed in, **My gallery**. Tap a photo to see where and when it was taken, the Kp at the time and the camera settings.

Tap **Beautiful** on photos you like. Beautiful votes earn the photographer points.

## Post a photo

1. In **Gallery**, tap **Post**.
2. **Choose a photo** you took.
3. Add a **Caption** if you like.
4. Under **Show where it was taken as**, choose **Town** (to about 10 km), **Wider area**, or **Don't show a place**.
5. Confirm that you took the photo and that it follows the community rules, then tap **Post photo**.

![Post a photo: the caption and how the place is shown](screen:gallery-post)

Location data is removed from the file before it's uploaded. A moderator checks each photo before it appears in the gallery; until then only you can see it, marked **Waiting for review**. You can post 10 photos a day.

## Rules

Post only aurora and night-sky photos you took yourself. Tap **Report photo** on anything that breaks the rules; a photo reported by three members is hidden until it's reviewed. **Block** hides a member's photos from you.
