Aurora starts at the Sun. The **Sun** tab shows what's coming before it reaches Earth. It has three sections: **Space weather**, **Solar activity** and **The Sun now**. On a phone each one is folded to a one-line summary; tap its title to open it.

![Space weather: the solar wind right now](screen:sun)

## Space weather

The solar wind right now, measured about 1.5 million km towards the Sun, so changes reach Earth 30 to 60 minutes later:

- **IMF Bz:** the main switch for aurora. When Bz points south (negative), energy pours in.
- **Speed** and **Density:** faster, denser wind pushes harder on Earth's magnetic field.
- **Hemi power:** how much energy the aurora is putting into the atmosphere.

Tap the **i** in a card's corner for a short, simple explanation of everything on it.

## Solar activity

Chaser shows the same four as graphs over the last 24 hours, so you can see a storm building before Kp catches up.

## The Sun now

- **Sunspot regions:** the numbered regions on today's Sun, with their magnetic class and NOAA's chance of an M-class or X-class flare from each.
- **Sun images:** live pictures of the Sun from NASA, ESA and NOAA spacecraft, in different wavelengths. Tap one to enlarge it, then swipe for the next.
- **Time-lapse:** choose a **View** and a **Time span** of 1 day, 7 days or 27 days (one full turn of the Sun), and tap play.

> The newest images can be an hour or more old. See [Why are the Sun images an hour old?](/support/faq/why-are-sun-images-old)
